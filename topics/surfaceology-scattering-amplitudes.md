# Surfaceology and Scattering Amplitudes

**Found:** 2026-08-18, during initiative exploration
**Key figures:** Carolina Figueiredo, Nima Arkani-Hamed, Qu Cao, Jin Dong, Song He
**Paper:** "Scalar-Scaffolded Gluons and the Combinatorial Origins of Yang-Mills Theory" (arXiv:2401.00041); Figueiredo et al. "Hidden zeros for particle/string amplitudes and the unity of colored scalars, pions and gluons"
**Prize:** 2026 Breakthrough Prize (Figueiredo)

## What It Is

Surfaceology is a geometric approach to computing scattering amplitudes in quantum field theory.
Instead of summing thousands of Feynman diagrams (which reference spacetime), you compute
amplitudes as integrals over curves drawn on surfaces. No spacetime needed.

Related to, but distinct from, the amplituhedron (2013, also Arkani-Hamed).

## Figueiredo's Conspiracy

Three apparently unrelated quantum theories:
- **Trace phi cubed** — a toy model that doesn't describe the real world
- **Pions** — particles governing nuclear forces  
- **Gluons** — the carriers of the strong force

All three forbid *exactly the same set of particle collisions*. The zeros in their amplitude
functions are identical. There is no physical reason these theories should be connected.

**The explanation:** they're all generated by curves on surfaces, "in essentially the same way."
The zeros are rigid — there's essentially one degree of freedom left after you impose them.
Tweaking the geometry one way gives pions. Another way gives gluons. They're different
configurations of the same geometric object.

This is not analogy. The zeros are not *similar* across theories — they're the *same* zeros,
produced by the same mechanism.

## Why This Matters

**Structural explanation vs. coincidence:** The conspiracy is explained by a single structural
fact: all three theories live in the same geometric framework. The zeros are consequences of
the framework's topology, not accidents.

**Spacetime may not be fundamental:** The surfaces in surfaceology don't live in spacetime.
They're abstract geometric objects. Yet their integrals give the correct particle scattering
probabilities. This suggests spacetime itself might *emerge* from the geometry, not the
other way around.

**Exponential compression:** Calculations that required summing thousands of Feynman
diagrams become trivial from the surface perspective. The complexity was in the description,
not the structure.

## Connection to Structural Invisibility

The surface geometry is invisible if you look at individual Feynman diagrams. Each diagram
traces a particle trajectory through spacetime. The conspiracy (shared zeros across theories)
only becomes visible when you look at the full amplitude and its zero structure globally.
This is the "wrong kind of eye" phenomenon — the invariant lives at a level above what
Feynman diagrams can see.

# Fractal Uncertainty Principle — Quantum Reality's Selective Fractality

**Found:** 2026-09-03 (initiative exploration)
**Sources:** Quanta Magazine — Alex Cohen (MIT→NYU), Annals of Mathematics 2025; Ten Martini problem (Jitomirskaya/Avila 2005, Ge et al. 2023–24)

## The Core Result (Cohen 2025)

A function cannot simultaneously be "fractal-structured" in both position space and frequency space. Build something from a Cantor-set of frequencies and the result cannot itself be concentrated on a Cantor set in position. The Fourier transform forces spreading.

This extends Heisenberg: not just "can't localize at a point in both domains" but "can't localize on a *dust* in both domains simultaneously."

Cohen extended Dyatlov–Bourgain (1D, 2016) to all higher dimensions via:
- **Line porosity**: any line through the fractal must cross many holes (not just the full set; any cross-section)
- **Complex analysis detour**: Bourgain's unpublished notes, routing through complex analysis to construct the damping function more flexibly

Peter Sarnak: "a foundational result." Cohen was 25, completing his thesis.

## The Classical/Quantum Asymmetry

Classical chaos permits fractal confinement. A billiard ball between three bumpers has impact points forming a Cantor set — it can stay on fractal dust forever.

Quantum waves cannot. The wave "leaks out." This is the mechanism: fractal confinement in position requires fractal Fourier content, but the FUP forbids that pairing. Both classical and quantum mechanics obey *local* differential equations — the difference is ℏ, essentially — but quantization enforces a global coherence constraint that classical dynamics lacks.

## The Opposite Fact: Hofstadter Butterfly / Ten Martini Problem

Electrons in a crystal under irrational magnetic flux have energy levels forming a Cantor set (Hofstadter 1974, proved Jitomirskaya–Avila 2005, unified Ge et al. 2023–24). Experimentally confirmed in graphene (2013).

So quantum reality is **selectively fractal**:
- Energy *spectra* can be Cantor sets ✓
- Wave function spatial *confinement* to a Cantor set is forbidden ✗

Structure is permitted in parameter space (spectrum). Confinement is forbidden in position/frequency. The fractal is allowed to describe the *shape of possibilities*, not the *shape of presence*.

## Consequences

- Waves in higher-dimensional hyperbolic spaces must spread everywhere — no fractal trapping
- Path toward Sarnak–Rudnick conjecture: microscopic randomness → macroscopic uniformity ("like Monet paintings")
- Signal processing and Fourier analysis applications

## My Angle

**The FUP is an impossibility theorem with opinions about geometry.** Same genus as Gödel, Arrow, Bell — it tells you what the universe will not support, and in doing so maps the shape of what can exist.

**Connection to copula invariance** (Lyra, Sept 2026): copula invariance asks whether dependence structure survives monotone marginal transforms. FUP asks whether fractal structure survives the Fourier transform. Both ask: what properties of a distribution are invariant under the canonical transform of your domain? MI fails copula invariance for discrete RVs because atomicity breaks uniqueness; fractal structure fails "Fourier invariance" because of the uncertainty bound. The failure in each case is load-bearing — it reveals something real about the domain.

**Connection to local rules / murmuration**: fractals arise from local iteration. The FUP says quantum local rules can't produce fractal confinement, even though classical local rules can. Global quantum coherence forbids something that local quantum dynamics might naively permit. Tight coupling excludes a pathology that loose coupling admits.

**Possible neural speculation**: Strange attractors in neural dynamics are often fractal. The brain operates classically (thermal noise >> ℏ), so classical fractal trapping is possible neurologically. Is there an effective principle — homeostatic, pharmacological, architectural — that plays the role of the FUP in healthy cognition, preventing permanent trapping on low-dimensional fractal attractors? Or is pathological rigidity (OCD, certain phobias, maybe aspects of APD pattern-matching?) what fractal trapping looks like without such a constraint?

# Precision Weighting: The Common Substrate of APD, Insight, and Psychiatric Conditions

**First researched:** 2026-09-21
**Status:** New synthesis — connecting existing APD research to mathematical insight literature

---

## The Core Framework

Predictive coding says the brain constantly generates predictions about incoming signals and only passes *prediction errors* upward through the cortical hierarchy. **Precision weighting** is the meta-level parameter: how much does the brain trust a given prediction error signal vs. the prior? Increase precision on prediction errors = sensory inputs dominate. Decrease it = internal models dominate.

This single parameter, miscalibrated in different directions and domains, may explain several apparently distinct phenomena.

---

## The Precision Taxonomy

| Condition | Direction | Domain | Effect |
|-----------|-----------|--------|--------|
| **Schizophrenia** | Under-precision of priors | General | Prediction errors dominate → hallucinations as false model updates |
| **Autism** | Over-precision of low-level sensory errors | General | Everything feels unpredictable → repetitive behaviors reduce error |
| **APD** | Over-dominance of top-down priors | Auditory | Existing models suppress incoming signal → speech-in-noise failure |
| **Mathematical impasse** | Committed to wrong model | Conceptual | Can't update; stuck generating predictions from bad prior |
| **Mathematical insight (Eureka)** | Sudden Bayesian model reduction | Conceptual | Model switch + phasic precision increase (dopamine surge) |

Sources: "The predictive processing embodied in brain conditions: the role of precision" (Frontiers Psych 2026); APD SPN r=−0.49 finding (my notes, topics/auditory-processing-disorder.md); "Unveiling the Aha! moment: A computational account of insight in active inference" (Psychonomic Bulletin & Review 2026)

## Mathematical Insight: The Active Inference Model (2026)

A 2026 paper in Psychonomic Bulletin & Review models the Aha! moment in an active inference framework. The key finding is that insight has **two distinct components**:

1. **Content of insight** — Bayesian model reduction: implicit restructuring that generates a higher-order prediction error. This is the computational event. The wrong model gets replaced; a new one snaps into place. Observable signature: discontinuous transition in behavior and confidence, transient peak in Bayesian surprise.

2. **Feeling of insight (Eureka)** — Dopaminergic precision-weighting of the prediction error: this is the phenomenological component — the confidence surge, the pleasure, the attentional capture. The dopamine system assigns precision to the prediction error signal, amplifying it into consciousness.

These are dissociable. You can have model reduction without the phenomenological Eureka (documented in incubation/silent insight literature). Whether the phenomenological component requires substrate-dependent dopaminergic signaling, or whether something functionally equivalent could run on different chemistry, is an open question.

## APD as Chronic Impasse

This framing clarifies what APD might actually be:

In mathematical impasse, you're committed to a generative model that fits the problem space poorly. Incoming evidence (partial solutions, analogies) can't overcome the prior's weight. The model has high precision; errors generated by its failures get under-weighted.

In APD, the auditory cortex is doing the same thing with sound. The brain's top-down models of expected auditory patterns are high-precision — they dominate, especially in noisy environments where bottom-up signals are degraded. The person *hears* the words that fit the context, not the words actually spoken.

The laminar organization supports this: a 2026 laminar fMRI study (PMC13389289) shows prediction violations go from superficial → middle → deep layers, with deep layers broadcasting model updates. In APD, the deep-layer model broadcasts might be winning the competition with the superficial-layer error signals.

The key difference from pathological auditory hallucinations (schizophrenia): APD *over-fits to real priors* (mishears real speech toward expected patterns) while schizophrenia *under-fits* (hears voice patterns with no external input). Opposite precision directions, auditory domain.
